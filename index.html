What Sentry Compliance Does

Compliance Infrastructure for Pharmacy Cold Storage

Sentry Compliance provides fully managed cold chain temperature monitoring for independent pharmacies in Melbourne. The service includes wireless sensors, a dedicated cellular gateway, SMS and email alerts, monthly TGA-aligned compliance reports, annual calibration support, and 5-year monitoring data retention.

Who It’s For

Independent pharmacy owners, pharmacists-in-charge, and pharmacy managers operating one or more sites across Melbourne metro.

The Problem It Solves

Manual temperature logs leave gaps overnight, on weekends, and on public holidays. They also cannot send alerts or produce the documented evidence chain needed for audits.

How Alerts Work

When a sensor detects a reading outside the configured range, SMS and email alerts are sent immediately to nominated contacts. If unacknowledged, alerts escalate to a secondary contact.

Why Cellular Matters

The monitoring gateway uses a cellular network connection, not your pharmacy WiFi. Router changes, internet outages, and password resets do not affect monitoring continuity.

Manual Logs Leave Dangerous Gaps

Most pharmacies work hard to maintain vaccine cold chain records. The problem is that manual checks and basic loggers often fail at the exact time you need them most — overnight, on weekends, during public holidays, or when no one is watching the fridge.

The Weekend Gap

A manual logbook may show a Friday afternoon reading and a Monday morning reading, but it cannot prove what happened during the 60+ hours in between.

No Alert, No Action

If a fridge compressor fails at 2am, a paper logbook cannot notify anyone. By the time staff arrive, vaccines may already need to be quarantined and assessed.

Weak Audit Evidence

Temperature data without calibration records, alert history, and response evidence may not provide the documentation needed during an audit, accreditation review, or insurance inquiry.

Cold Chain Compliance Is an Operational Risk, Not an Admin Task

Australian vaccine cold chain guidance is built around keeping vaccines within the recommended +2°C to +8°C range, with +5°C as the target midpoint under the National Vaccine Storage Guidelines “Strive for 5”. When monitoring is manual, disconnected, or uncalibrated, the risk is not just product loss — it is incomplete evidence.

An audit, accreditation review, or insurance inquiry does not simply ask whether your fridge was cold enough. It asks whether you can demonstrate, with documented records, that you knew continuously what the temperature was — and that you acted when it changed.

Sentry Compliance turns those evidence points into a monthly report your team can file before anyone asks for it.

What Happens During a Temperature Excursion

When a sensor detects a reading outside its configured threshold, the system immediately alerts nominated contacts via SMS and email. If the first contact does not acknowledge within the configured period, the alert escalates to a secondary contact. Every excursion event is recorded and included in the monthly compliance report.
